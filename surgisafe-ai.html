⚙ How It Works

From sensor to alert
in under two seconds

1

Sensor Data Collection

Patient vitals are captured continuously via bedside monitors — heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, temperature — fed in real time to the AI engine.

2

Surgical Video Capture

High-definition cameras record the operative field. Computer vision models analyze the footage frame-by-frame for procedural anomalies.

3

AI Multi-Stream Processing

Both data streams are fused and processed by our predictive model, which correlates events across sensors and video to build a holistic risk score.

4

Anomaly & Risk Detection

When deviations cross clinical thresholds, the system classifies the situation as Normal, Warning, or Critical — instantly and automatically.

5

Surgical Team Alert

Color-coded visual and audio alerts are delivered to the OR dashboard immediately. The system also surfaces guideline-based action suggestions to assist decision-making — never to replace clinical judgment.

🚨 Smart Alert System

Triage intelligence,
built into every alert

Three-tier color-coded alerts with audio signals ensure the right response at the right moment — no ambiguity, no delay.

🟢

Normal

All vitals and surgical parameters are within expected ranges. No intervention required. System continues passive monitoring.

🟡

Warning

One or more parameters trending toward risk thresholds. Surgical team is notified to increase attention. AI surfaces early recommendations.

🔴

Critical

Immediate clinical action required. High-priority audio and visual alerts are triggered simultaneously. Guideline-based emergency support activates.
